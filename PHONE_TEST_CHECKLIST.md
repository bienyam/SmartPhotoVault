# Physical iPhone test checklist

Nothing that removes or copies photos has been proven on a real iPhone yet. Run these on the TestFlight build before trusting any of it with real memories. Use throwaway items you create for the test, never real photos.

TestFlight: builds now reach the **TidyAway Testers** group automatically. Install the latest build from the TestFlight app.

## Before you start
1. Take 6 throwaway photos (point at a wall), 2 screenshots, and one 30-second video.
2. Duplicate two of the photos in Photos (select, Share, Duplicate).
3. Favorite one of the throwaway photos.
4. Note your free space in Settings, General, iPhone Storage.

## 1. Scan
- [ ] Home, Check my photos, Scan Apple Photos. The Photos permission prompt appears only after tapping Scan.
- [ ] Clean tab, Find exact duplicates. The scan finishes and shows progress; Cancel stops it.
- [ ] The Ledger shows the scan, with no file names.

## 2. Duplicate removal
- [ ] Review duplicate groups shows your two duplicated photos. The favorite is listed first and marked to keep.
- [ ] The delete button stays off until the sign-off box is checked.
- [ ] Delete: Apple's own confirmation appears. Tap Don't Allow first: nothing is removed and the Ledger says so.
- [ ] Repeat and allow: the extra copies move to Recently Deleted; the original and the favorite stay.

## 3. Clean categories and category removal
- [ ] After a fresh scan, Look through by type shows your screenshots and video with sizes.
- [ ] In Screenshots, nothing is pre-selected; the favorite (if any) can't be selected.
- [ ] Select one test screenshot, check the sign-off, delete, allow Apple's prompt. Only that screenshot moves to Recently Deleted.
- [ ] Bursts show no select buttons.
- [ ] "Maybe saved from chats or the web": check it doesn't list your own camera photos too often.

## 4. Archive copy, verify and restore
- [ ] Archive tab: connect a folder (Google Drive in Files, or your home server via Connect to Server).
- [ ] Pick two test photos and the test video. Sizes show; the space check passes.
- [ ] Sign off and copy. Every item reports verified; the files appear in the folder.
- [ ] Turn off Wi-Fi halfway through a home-server copy: the copy stops with a clear message and nothing is half-saved.
- [ ] Test restore to a different folder: the restored file opens and matches.
- [ ] Archived tab lists the copies; restore one back.
- [ ] Album and place filters: pick an album and an area on the map; suggestions match. Suggest large videos lists your test video if it's over 25 MB.

## 5. Pro
- [ ] Home, TidyAway Pro opens. With the sandbox account, the price shows, Unlock works, and Restore finds it.
- [ ] After unlocking, Ledger, What Pro adds shows Pro as unlocked.

## 6. Afterwards
- [ ] Empty the test items from Recently Deleted yourself if you want the space back.
- [ ] Compare free space with step 4 of Before you start.
- [ ] Note anything confusing or wrong and send it back.
