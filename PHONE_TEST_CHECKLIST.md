# Physical iPhone test checklist

Nothing that removes or copies photos has been proven on a real iPhone yet. Run these on the latest TestFlight build before trusting it with real memories. Use throwaway items you create for the test, never real photos.

TestFlight: every upload reaches the **TidyAway Testers** group automatically. It is the only tester group. Install the latest build from the TestFlight app.

## Before you start
1. Take 6 throwaway photos (point at a wall), 2 screenshots, and one 30-second video.
2. Duplicate two of the photos in Photos (select, Share, Duplicate).
3. Favorite one of the throwaway photos.
4. Note your free space in Settings, General, iPhone Storage, and compare it with the bar at the top of Free up.

## 1. Free up
- [ ] The app opens on Free up with one "Free up space" button. No Photos prompt until you tap it.
- [ ] Tap Free up space and allow access. Rows appear within seconds (Large videos first) while the check continues; Stop keeps earlier results.
- [ ] Large videos lists your test video with its size, largest first.
- [ ] More, Activity shows the check, with no file names.

## 2. Duplicates
- [ ] Duplicates shows your two duplicated photos. One copy in each group says "Keeping this one"; the favorite is kept.
- [ ] Nothing is selected until you tap. Select all selects only the extra copies.
- [ ] Delete stays off until the sign-off box is ticked. The (i) next to it shows the full wording.
- [ ] Delete: Apple's confirmation appears once for the whole batch. Tap Don't Allow first: nothing is removed and the bar says so.
- [ ] Repeat and allow: the "Moved to Recently Deleted" screen appears with the 3 steps. The extra copies are gone from the list without a new check.

## 3. Screenshots and other types
- [ ] Screenshots lists your test screenshots. Select one, sign off, delete, allow. Only that screenshot moves to Recently Deleted.
- [ ] More types: each type opens; Bursts has no select buttons.
- [ ] "Saved from chats (a guess)": check it doesn't list your own camera photos too often.
- [ ] Follow the 3 steps to empty Recently Deleted, come back, and check the storage bar shows more free space.

## 4. Archive to a drive
- [ ] Archive, Move out: the write-only line is shown.
- [ ] Plug in a USB-C stick or drive. Choose "Drive plugged into this iPhone", then the drive in Files. TidyAway says "Ready" with the free space, or explains an NTFS or read-only drive.
- [ ] If you have an NTFS drive: the message explains Erase as ExFAT and warns it wipes the drive. Copy is blocked.
- [ ] Pick two test photos and the test video. Sign off and copy. Every item reports verified; the files appear on the drive.
- [ ] Unplug the drive halfway through a copy: the copy stops with a clear message and nothing half-saved is left.
- [ ] Get back: the copies are listed. Restore one to a different folder; it opens and matches.
- [ ] The free archive meter shows how much of the 5 GB is left.
- [ ] After a copy, the full-screen Archived screen appears. See them in Google Drive opens the TidyAway Archive folder.
- [ ] Free up space on iPhone: sign off, allow Apple's prompt; only the archived test items go to Recently Deleted and favorites stay.
- [ ] Get back lists the archive by date; open the date, select an item, put it back to Photos; it appears in Photos and opens.

## 5. Pro (sandbox account)
- [ ] More, TidyAway Pro shows Unlock and Restore straight away.
- [ ] Unlock with the sandbox account. "Pro is unlocked." appears without leaving the screen.
- [ ] Album and place filters on Archive, and Advanced analysis under Analyze storage, open without the Pro screen.
- [ ] Delete and reinstall the app, then Restore purchase: Pro comes back.

## 6. Afterwards
- [ ] Empty the test items from Recently Deleted yourself if you haven't.
- [ ] Note anything confusing or wrong and send it back.
