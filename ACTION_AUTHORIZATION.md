# Consequential actions and user authorization

Draft for review by qualified counsel before shipping any deletion, transfer, or paid storage feature. This is product authorization copy.

## Review and confirm

Before approval, show the exact action, selected items, source and destination accounts, full-resolution copy that will remain, storage and cost implications, and recovery deadline if known. Use an unchecked **I understand and authorize these changes** control, an action-specific confirmation button, and a visible **Cancel** button. Open any required system or provider confirmation separately.

### Delete from Apple Photos

> I authorize Smart Photo Vault Inventory to delete [count] selected items from Apple Photos. If iCloud Photos is on, these items will also be deleted from iCloud Photos and synced devices. Apple normally keeps deleted items in Recently Deleted for up to 30 days unless they’re permanently deleted sooner. A verified full-resolution copy will remain in [destination and account], last checked [time]. [If applicable] This leaves one full-resolution copy. If that copy is lost, you could lose these items permanently.

Button **Delete [count] items from Apple Photos**. Use accurate provider-specific effects and recovery periods for any other provider.

### Delete reviewed items from Apple Photos (Clean categories)

Used when someone deletes items they picked one by one from a Clean category (screenshots, GIFs, Live Photos, slo-mo, time-lapse, panoramas, or the "Maybe saved from chats or the web" guess). Nothing is pre-selected, favorites can't be selected, and bursts stay review-only.

> I authorize TidyAway to delete [count] items I selected from [category] in Apple Photos. These items are not duplicates: unless I archived them elsewhere, Apple Photos holds the only copy. If iCloud Photos is on, they will also be deleted from iCloud Photos and synced devices. Apple normally keeps deleted items in Recently Deleted for up to 30 days unless they're permanently deleted sooner; after that they can't be recovered.

Button **Delete [count] items from Apple Photos**.

### Copy to cloud storage

> I authorize copying [count] items, including [files and metadata], to [provider and account]. The transfer will use approximately [size] and may cost [amount or pricing basis]. Existing copies stay where they are.

Button **Copy [count] items to [provider]**. Ask separately before removing any source copy.

### Restore or change billing

- **Restore [count] items to [location]** — Show the destination, whether restoration adds copies, expected transfer size, wait time, and charges.
- **Start plan at [price] per [billing period]** — Show total price, renewal, cancellation, provider charges, and retrieval or exit fees before confirmation.

## Evidence and execution

- Bind authorization to the exact items and operation, disclosure version, source and destination, verification evidence, timestamp, and an operation ID. A change in scope, destination, cost, or evidence requires fresh approval. Verify the destination again immediately before removing anything.
- Record consent and execution separately. Show a receipt with what succeeded, failed, or was cancelled. Preserve exact evidence only as long as necessary, with a documented retention and deletion policy. Avoid retaining photos, file names, or credentials in receipts.
- Block removal when a destination cannot be verified or restored, when permissions change, or when the effects on shared items, edits, albums, or favorites are uncertain. No permanent purge in the initial release.
- Obtain legal review of the wording, receipt and retention design, jurisdiction-specific requirements, billing disclosures, and whether a formal electronic signature is needed. Update the privacy policy and App Store disclosures as storage and data handling change.

The present app scans resources already available on the iPhone. Any future retrieval of a cloud original, photo deletion, or provider upload requires its own review and authorization.
