TidyAway product vision

From cleanup tools to a one-scan storage coach. Built from three Fable 5.2 research reports (market and user voice, behaviour science, on-device AI feasibility) and your direction, 11/21/2026. Nothing here is built yet.

"One scan finds the junk you didn't know you were keeping. One tap clears it, safely."

Today TidyAway is a set of tools: a duplicate finder, type lists, an archive form, a cost calculator. People have to know what to do with each. The apps that win this category do the opposite. They scan once, show a big "you can free X GB" number, and turn every finding into a ready-made action.

Our edge is to do that honestly: on-device, fair pricing, never deleting the wrong thing, and space that is actually freed. The whole category is seen as predatory, and that's the gap we take.

2. How people behave, and what that means for design

FindingDesign rule for TidyAway
About 70% of phone photos are never looked at again. People keep things "just in case" or "as evidence". Curation only happens when storage runs out.Meet people at the "Storage Full" moment, and get them a first win in under 60 seconds.
People accept deletion suggestions far more when the reason is shown and can be checked (Göbel 2022).Every suggestion says why: "Same as this one. We kept the sharper one." Show it side by side.
People trust an imperfect algorithm if they can adjust it even slightly (Dietvorst 2018).Suggestions come ready to go, with one-tap overrides: "Keep all from this day" and "Never suggest this again".
Choice overload only hurts when the choice is unclear.Batch the obvious (duplicates, black frames, old screenshots). Swipe the ambiguous (similar shots, memes), in short sessions of about 20.
A head start makes people finish (Nunes & Drèze 2006).Open with progress already made: "We already found 2.1 GB of exact copies."
Age alone is the wrong signal: old memories gain value, old screenshots lose it."Old" means age × type. Ask once with presets (1 / 3 / 5 years), show the live count, and default to the most cautious.
Repeated "are you sure?" prompts feed anxiety.Mention the safety net once: "Goes to Recently Deleted for 30 days, or archive a copy first." Then finish the job by emptying Recently Deleted.
Charging at the Delete button feels like a toll.Charge for labour saved, never for the exit. Finding junk and deleting it by hand stay free.

How a finding becomes an action

  1. One scan: details first (seconds), then a check of small previews in the background. Results fill in as they're found, biggest first. It works with Optimize iPhone Storage on, because previews are enough.
  2. Grouped by confidence: Sure means certain by rule, Likely means the AI is fairly confident, Your call means it depends on you.
  3. Each group has one action: Clear, Review (swipe), Make smaller, or Move to archive.
  4. Every item says why it was suggested, and favorites and photos you've edited are always kept.
  5. Finish line: "Empty Recently Deleted to get 3.4 GB back now", then the storage bar updates to prove it.
  6. Coming back: an optional monthly tidy-up. Only the new photos are checked, so it takes seconds.

• Make smaller: shrink big videos and high-resolution photos, with the original archived

★ Pricing: keep $39.99 lifetime and $7.99 a month, and add a yearly plan at about $24.99. Weekly-style pricing is the category's top complaint. Annual plans keep up to about 10 times more subscribers after a year than weekly ones (RevenueCat), and a yearly plan is what most people pick. Never weekly.

Why Pro is obviously worth it: free shows "8.4 GB of junk". Free clears the 3.4 GB that's certain. Pro clears the remaining 5 GB in one tap, safely. That's paying for time saved, not for permission to delete.
